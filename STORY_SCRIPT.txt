1. A small town school
For years, a quiet student known as Noob was laughed at because his clothes were old and his family had very little money.

2. The fee announcement
One morning, the school owner announced that tuition fees would triple. Many families could no longer afford to keep their children in school.

3. Noob speaks up
Noob stepped forward. He said that a family's income should never decide whether a child gets an education.

4. A promise
Some students laughed again, but Noob stayed calm. He promised that he would find a way to help every student learn.

5. The next morning
The next morning, a line of shining cars arrived outside the school. Everyone stopped and stared.

6. The big reveal
A visitor introduced Noob as the founder of the Bright Future Education Foundation. The students could hardly believe it.

7. A new school
Noob used the foundation to build a bright, modern school. Every student could study there without paying tuition.

8. Everything included
Books, uniforms, classrooms and learning supplies were provided free. No child would be turned away because of money.

9. A better ending
The bullies apologized. Noob forgave them. He had never wanted revenge. He wanted equal opportunities for everyone.
