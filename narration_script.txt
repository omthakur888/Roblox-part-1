NARRATION SCRIPT — THE TOWN WHERE THE FLOOR IS LAVA

Imagine living in a town where nobody has touched the floor for generations!

Odyson was born and raised here. Beds floated with thrusters, people travelled on air bikes, and even school chairs hovered above the ground.

But one day, Odyson started questioning everything. Was the floor really dangerous?

He was too scared to test it himself, so he tried to convince Maggit, a quiet kid in his class, to step down.

Maggit refused. During their argument, Odyson accidentally pushed him, and they both lost their balance.

Maggit's emergency thrusters activated just in time. But Odyson kept falling!

Everyone watched in shock. Odyson closed his eyes, expecting the worst.

But when he opened them, he was perfectly safe.

The floor wasn't lava. It was just an ordinary floor!

An entire generation had followed a rule without ever testing it.

Traditions can be worth respecting, but blindly following them without questioning can stop us from discovering the truth.
